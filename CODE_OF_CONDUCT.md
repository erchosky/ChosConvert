# Code of Conduct

ChosConvert follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and keep discussions
focused on the work. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Report incidents privately to the
maintainers (see SECURITY.md for contact); reports are handled confidentially.
